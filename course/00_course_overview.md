# The NFT Investor's Playbook — course overview

Bored Capital Academy, operated by BlockRock OÜ. Independent of Yuga Labs, BAYC and any employer.
Tagline: Think Beyond the Floor.

## Core promise
Learners finish able to distinguish quoted NFT prices from executable value, analyse market
liquidity and ownership, and assess collectibles using documented evidence rather than hype.

## Format (self-paced edition)
- Module 1: Why NFT prices are misleading (liquidity, price formation, trading quality, ownership, BAYC walkthrough)
- Exercise: apply the Ape Analytics Scorecard to a public NFT collection
- Module 2: What makes a collectible valuable? (valuation frameworks, BAYC economics, Mudwater case, comparison exercise)
- Materials: scorecard workbook, case-study notes, glossary, source list
- Assessment: a one-page collection assessment
- Premium tier: live Q&A sessions with the founder

## Learning outcomes
1. Separate floor, best bid, observed sale and estimated liquid exit value.
2. Construct basic NFT turnover and holder-concentration diagnostics.
3. Understand why collectible fair value is uncertain and often non-cash-flow based.
4. Recognise what changes when physical redemption, sealed packs and rarity are involved.
5. Produce a short evidence-based collection note with key assumptions and unknowns.

## What the academy is — and is not
In scope: educational valuation frameworks and historical examples; market structure, liquidity and
ownership analysis; BAYC and Mudwater as transparent case studies; workbook, methods, reproducible research.
Out of scope: personalised buy, sell or hold recommendations; client portfolio reviews or discretionary
asset management; claims of official BAYC/Yuga endorsement; promised profits, price predictions or
proprietary "signals".
