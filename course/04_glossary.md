# Glossary

- **Floor**: cheapest live listing (an ask).
- **Best bid / top collection offer**: highest live offer valid for any token in the collection.
- **Bid/ask spread**: (floor − best bid) / floor.
- **Listed float**: share of supply currently listed for sale.
- **Exit curve**: average proceeds from selling N tokens into the current bid book, best bid first.
- **Slippage / liquidity discount**: how far realised proceeds fall below the quoted floor.
- **Headline market cap**: floor × supply. Not realisable cash.
- **Turnover**: distinct tokens traded in a window / supply.
- **Wash trading**: trades between related parties that inflate volume or price signals.
- **HHI**: Herfindahl–Hirschman index, sum of squared ownership shares ×10,000; higher = more concentrated.
- **Contract-held share**: tokens held by smart contracts (vaults, escrow, lending), whose real owners are hidden.
- **Comparable sales (comps)**: recent completed sales used as reference points; rarity and timing matter.
- **Data confidence**: how much the evidence can be trusted given gaps, truncation and caveats.
