# Module 2 — What makes a collectible valuable?

## Valuation frameworks
Comparable sales, rarity, liquidity discounts and scenario ranges. There is no single "correct price".
Learners state an indicative range, describe the conditions underpinning it and identify where data
quality prevents a conclusion.

## BAYC economics
BAYC is an established PFP/brand ecosystem where market liquidity and membership or IP benefits must be
analysed separately: membership, network effects, IP use rights under the holder licence, brand, and
other non-cash-flow benefits. Check the official licence terms rather than assuming rights.

## Mudwater case study
A collectible model involving a relationship between digital and physical packs/cards: digital and
physical backing, pack opening, supply, rarity and trade dynamics. Use published rules and current,
verified data rather than unsupported claims about circulation, redemption rights or market capitalisation.

## Comparison exercise
Compare two collections with the scorecard; distinguish evidence from opinion.

## Debrief
Summarise uncertainties and document what further data would change the view.
