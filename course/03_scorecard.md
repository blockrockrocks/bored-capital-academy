# The Ape Analytics Scorecard

A structured worksheet that requires evidence, sources and documented uncertainty — not a magic alpha signal.

| Dimension | Weight | Evidence examples |
|---|---|---|
| Liquidity and exit depth | 30% | Best bid, live listings, recent sale sizes, bid/ask gap, slippage |
| Trading activity and quality | 20% | Volume, turnover, active trading days, suspicious patterns |
| Ownership and supply | 20% | Holder count, wallets-per-holder caveats, concentration, listed float |
| Benefits, rights and use cases | 15% | Documented utilities, membership, licensing conditions, actual usage |
| Execution and transparency | 15% | Communication, delivered milestones, disclosures, data quality |

Scoring: rate each dimension 0–5 using the written criteria; the workbook computes a weighted 0–100 score
for discussion. Always read it next to the data-confidence indicator. No score is a target price, risk
limit or recommendation.

Written criteria (anchors):
- Liquidity: 0 = no meaningful bids / listings far apart; 3 = moderate spread, bid book absorbs a few tokens with modest discount; 5 = tight spread, deep funded bids, low slippage for 10+ tokens.
- Trading: 0 = sporadic or mostly flagged trades; 3 = regular trading, some concentration in few wallets; 5 = trades most days, broad buyer/seller base, negligible wash flags.
- Ownership: 0 = few wallets control most supply or large opaque contract holdings; 3 = some whales, caveats noted; 5 = broadly distributed, low listed float, concentration well understood.
- Benefits: 0 = undocumented or unused; 3 = documented with partial evidence of use; 5 = clear licence terms, verifiable ongoing use, benefits independent of price.
- Execution: 0 = missed milestones, poor disclosure; 3 = mixed record; 5 = consistent delivery, transparent communication and data.

Workbook tabs: Read me, Market snapshot, Ownership, Valuation scenarios, Scorecard, One-page report, Sources.

## The one-page assessment (graded)
Market snapshot, liquidity score, ownership observations, rights and utility summary, principal risks,
sources, assumptions and one unanswered question. Graded on completeness and reasoning — never on the
direction of a price prediction.
