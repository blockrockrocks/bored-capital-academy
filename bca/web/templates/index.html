FAQ

Questions

Will you tell me which NFTs to buy?

No. This is education. You learn a research method and apply it yourself. We don't give personal recommendations, price targets or signals, and neither does the AI tutor.

Do I need to connect a wallet?

No. There's no wallet connection and no token gating. You pay in euros by card or another standard method.

Is this official BAYC content?

No. Bored Capital Academy is independent and not affiliated with Yuga Labs or BAYC. BAYC is used as a public case study.

Who is it for?

Collectors who know the basics and want rigorous methods: BAYC and MAYC holders, multi-collection collectors and community organisers.

What's in the Live tier?

Group Q&A sessions with the founder and personal feedback on your one-page assessment, on top of everything in Self-paced.

Running a club or community?
